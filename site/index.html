    Electricity Market Pricing

    Observed Ontario wholesale electricity prices plus the Class B Global Adjustment (GA) for the latest 60 complete months: the legacy Hourly Ontario Energy Price (HOEP, retired April 30, 2025) up to April 2025, then the Ontario Price from May 1, 2025. Full cost = energy + Class B GA. For comparison only: not a tariff, a bill or a forecast.

    The Hourly Ontario Energy Price (HOEP) retired on April 30, 2025 (legacy market). Since May 1, 2025, market-billed customers pay the Ontario Electricity Market Price (the Ontario Price: Day-Ahead Ontario Zonal Price plus Load Forecast Deviation Adjustment) plus the Global Adjustment: Class B customers pay the actual monthly Class B rate per kWh, and Class A customers pay by their peak demand factor.
